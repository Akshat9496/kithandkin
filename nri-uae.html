What We Do

A concierge service built on
trust, not technology.

Your parents are home. You are not. And the only way you know they are okay is a two-minute phone call where they say "I’m fine." Kith & Kin changes that. We visit your parent at home on a regular schedule — check their vitals, confirm their medications, sit with them and have a proper conversation, and send you a full report the same day. Not a reassurance. A real report.

I

Structured Home Visits

Regular, scheduled in-person visits. Consistent, never missed, supervised to the same standard every time.

II

Health Monitoring

BP and pulse checked and logged every visit. Medication adherence observed. Early warning signs identified and flagged.

III

Family Kept Informed

Same-day WhatsApp report after every visit — vitals, medication, mood, and any issues flagged. You always know.

IV

Errands & Coordination

Bills, groceries, home repairs, banking, hospital appointments — we coordinate so nothing falls through the cracks.

Non-medical monitoring only. Kith & Kin is a companionship and concierge service — not a nursing agency or medical provider. We observe, report, coordinate and accompany. WhatsApp support available 7am–10pm daily.

Kith & Kin was founded by a Project Manager who spent years in London managing high-accountability programmes for major clients. He returned to India with his family and one clear purpose: to build the elder support service he wished had existed when he was abroad.

Every onboarding visit is personally supervised and a monthly check-in is conducted with every active client. This is not delegated. This is not automated. It is a deliberate choice to keep the client circle small enough that every family is genuinely known.

This is not a franchise or a call centre. When you message Kith & Kin, you get a direct response — not a call centre. When something goes wrong, our team responds immediately. That accountability is by design and it will not change.

On the same day as every visit, your family WhatsApp group receives a structured report: vitals, medication, mood observations, and issues flagged. Signature and Premier members also get proactive updates between visits.
Our team is the first call. We notify your family immediately, coordinate with your doctor or hospital, and stay on-site until stable. Premier members receive a written emergency protocol at onboarding.
