Elder care in Faridabad: what families need to know.

Kith & Kin — Elder Concierge5 min readFaridabad, Delhi NCR
← Back to all articles

Faridabad is one of Delhi NCR's most populous cities — and one of its most overlooked when it comes to professional elder care. While Gurgaon and South Delhi attract the majority of concierge and home care services, hundreds of thousands of elderly residents live across Faridabad's sectors: from NIT to Sector 15, from Ballabhgarh to the colonies around Old Faridabad.

If your parents live in Faridabad and you are abroad, this guide is for you.

The Faridabad elder care gap

Delhi NCR's elder care infrastructure is unevenly distributed. Most registered home nursing services and concierge care companies are concentrated in South Delhi and Gurgaon. Faridabad, despite its size and population, has fewer professional options — and the ones that exist are often focused on post-surgical nursing care, not the broader, holistic support that most elderly residents actually need.

What most elderly parents in Faridabad need is not a nurse. It is:

  • Someone to accompany them to a hospital appointment without family having to travel from abroad
  • A regular visitor who checks on their medication, mobility, and mood
  • Someone to handle the unexpected — a bureaucratic issue, a delivery, a minor emergency — without involving the family in every small crisis
  • Honest, regular updates for the family abroad that go beyond "I'm fine"

Hospital and medical support in Faridabad

Faridabad has several good hospitals — ESIC Model Hospital, Metro Hospital, Fortis Escorts, QRG. But accompanying an elderly parent to these hospitals, managing appointments, waiting with them, and communicating findings back to a family in London or Toronto is genuinely difficult without local support.

What families often discover: appointments that were made are not attended because the parent does not want to burden relatives. Diagnoses are not properly understood or followed up. Prescriptions are filled once and not refilled.

A professional visitor who attends the appointment, listens, takes notes, and reports back transforms this picture entirely.

The medication problem

This is the most common concern we hear from NRI families with parents in Faridabad. Parents have multiple medications for blood pressure, diabetes, thyroid, or cardiac conditions. Without regular, accountable oversight, medication management often breaks down quietly.

The parent may:

  • Reduce dosage on their own because they feel the medication is making them tired
  • Stop a medication after a perceived side effect without consulting a doctor
  • Forget whether they have taken a dose and skip it to be safe
  • Run out of medication and delay refilling because the pharmacy trip feels difficult

None of these will show up on a Sunday video call. A regular home visit, with a medication check built in, catches these things early.

"Medication non-compliance is the single most common — and most reliably invisible — thing we find on first visits."

Social isolation in Faridabad's residential colonies

Faridabad's older residential colonies — many built in the 1970s and 80s — were designed for a generation that expected extended families to stay nearby. That social contract has changed. Many of those colonies now have a higher-than-average proportion of elderly residents whose children have moved to Delhi, Gurgaon, or abroad.

The result is a quiet epidemic of loneliness among elderly residents who have neighbours but no company, who are physically well but emotionally isolated. Loneliness at this level is a genuine health risk — associated with increased risk of cognitive decline, depression, and cardiovascular disease.

A regular, friendly, professional presence in the home is not a luxury for these residents. It is meaningful social engagement with someone who is genuinely interested in how they are doing.

What to look for in a Faridabad elder care service

If you are researching options for your parents in Faridabad, here is what to ask:

  • Does the service actually cover Faridabad's sectors, or only certain parts of NCR?
  • How are visits documented, and how quickly do you receive updates?
  • What happens in a non-medical emergency — a fall, a power cut, an unexpected issue?
  • Is there a consistent person your parent will come to know and trust, or does the team rotate?
  • Can the service accompany your parent to hospital appointments?

The most important question: Will someone I trust be building a genuine relationship with my parent? Not just completing visits — actually getting to know them.
